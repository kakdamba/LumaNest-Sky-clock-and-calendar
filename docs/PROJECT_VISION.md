# LumaNest — Project Vision & Core Principles

> **"LumaNest is a cozy, reliable, offline-first Sky companion that helps players know what is happening in Sky, coordinate with friends around the world, and never miss important events."**

- **Product Identity:** LumaNest (Unofficial fan-made companion for *Sky: Children of the Light*, not affiliated with or endorsed by thatgamecompany).
- **Core Tagline:** *"Your cozy companion for Sky."*
- **Primary Platform:** Android First (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room/SQLite, WorkManager, AppWidgets/Glance).
- **Mascots & Art Style:** Original characters (Boy in hoodie + fluffy white dog). Hand-drawn, cozy, dreamy, nighttime aesthetic. No ripped or copyrighted TGC assets.
- **Privacy & Storage:** Offline-first, strictly local friend storage, no forced accounts or tracking, opt-in calendar sync.
